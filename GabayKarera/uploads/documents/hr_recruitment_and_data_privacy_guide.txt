GABAYKARERA AI - HR RECRUITMENT & DATA PRIVACY COMPLIANCE POLICY

1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines the official data privacy standards, document security protocols, and ethical recruitment practices enforced by GabayKarera AI.

2. Candidate Data Management:
- Resumes, phone numbers, email addresses, and educational transcripts submitted by applicants are strictly protected under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
- HR Personnel may view candidate qualifications and AI competency scores strictly for legitimate evaluation purposes.

3. Automated Screening Oversight:
- AI competency matching scores serve as objective assistance to decision-makers. Final hiring authority remains with HR personnel and hiring managers.
